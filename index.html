Dear Hope,

I've started this letter more times than I can count, and every version felt too small for what I actually need to say. So I'll just say it plainly: I'm sorry. Not the quick kind of sorry we toss around to smooth things over, but the kind that comes from actually sitting with what I did and how it landed on you.

I know my words hurt you, and I know that no explanation changes that. I was careless with something that mattered — with you — and you deserved better from me. I keep replaying it, wishing I'd chosen differently, wishing I'd thought before I acted instead of being mad and mawala sa mood tapos nasasabayan na kita.

You don't owe me your forgiveness, and I'm not writing this to rush you toward it. I just wanted you to know that I see what I did, I understand why it hurt, and I'm not asking you to pretend it didn't happen. I'm only asking for the chance to do better — to show you, not just tell you.

Whatever you decide, please know this: you matter to me, more than I showed in that moment. I hope, in time, there's room for us to find our way back to each other.

With love and regret, Me
